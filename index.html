Picabo

一次性拼好多张截图，直接长图拼接，没有拼图画框限制画幅，横向纵向图都能一起拼。也支持边框选择和马赛克，还能处理圆角，能直接导出 PDF 与原图质量图片。

常见问题

图片会上传吗？
不会。Picabo 没有联网功能，所有处理都在手机上完成。

为什么只请求“添加到照片”权限？
选图用的是系统选择器，只有你勾选的图片会交给 Picabo；保存时才需要写入相册的权限。

导出的图片清晰吗？
可以选择原图质量导出，也可以导出 PDF。

Picabo

Stitch many screenshots into one long image at once, with no collage frame limiting the size. Portrait and landscape images can be stitched together. Frames, mosaic redaction and rounded corners are supported, and you can export a PDF or images at original quality.

FAQ

Are my images uploaded?
No. Picabo has no network features. Everything is processed on your phone.

Why does Picabo only ask for "Add to Photos" permission?
Images are chosen with the system picker, so only the ones you select reach Picabo. Write access is needed only to save results.

Is the export full quality?
You can export at original quality, or as a PDF.
