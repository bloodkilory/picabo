Picabo 隐私政策

生效日期：2026 年 11 月 26 日

Picabo 是一款在设备上完成全部处理的长图拼接工具。我们不收集、不上传、不分享你的任何数据。

我们不收集的数据

  • Picabo 不包含任何网络请求代码，不会把图片、文件或使用记录发送到任何服务器。
  • 没有账号、广告、统计分析或第三方 SDK。
  • 不收集设备标识、位置、联系人或其他个人信息。

照片的使用方式

  • 选图使用系统照片选择器，Picabo 只能读取你在选择器里勾选的图片，不能访问整个相册。
  • 拼接、马赛克、水印、边框和导出都在你的设备上完成。
  • 保存结果时，Picabo 会请求“添加到照片”的权限，只用于把你导出的图片写入相册，不会读取相册里的其他内容。
  • 导出 PDF 或分享时，文件通过系统分享面板交给你选择的应用，Picabo 不保留副本。

儿童隐私

Picabo 不收集任何人的个人信息，包括 13 岁以下的儿童。

政策变更

如果本政策有变化，我们会更新本页面并修改上方的生效日期。

Picabo Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 26, 2026

Picabo is a long-image stitching tool that does all of its work on your device. We do not collect, upload or share any of your data.

Data we do not collect

  • Picabo contains no networking code and never sends images, files or usage information to any server.
  • There are no accounts, ads, analytics or third-party SDKs.
  • No device identifiers, location, contacts or other personal information are collected.

How photos are used

  • Images are chosen with the system photo picker, so Picabo only sees the images you select, never your whole library.
  • Stitching, redaction, watermarks, frames and export all happen on your device.
  • When you save a result, Picabo asks for "Add to Photos" permission, used only to write the exported image to your library. It cannot read anything else in your library.
  • PDF export and sharing hand the file to the app you choose through the system share sheet. Picabo keeps no copy.

Children's privacy

Picabo collects no personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the effective date above.
